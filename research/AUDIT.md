# Pre-implementation technical audit
The delivered ZIPs are retained in baselines/. The BTP notebook and ml_engine pipeline apply EWMA smoothing twice around Isolation Forest anomaly replacement. Its app instead fits a line to the replaced raw readings. Both regress against entry number. The MTP frozen research engine matches the supplied BTP source byte-for-byte. Our new comparison uses the BTP app because that was its user-facing estimate. The inherited experiments compare both versions.

Original MTP architecture: input_parsing → validated dated WeightLog → energy_balance (Huber IRLS, gap-aware AR sandwich, rolling estimates and reliability factors) → tracker and report. ml_engine adapts results for agent_coach, which combines tools, local clinical retrieval and optional Gemini. lstm_forecaster includes older model artifacts and the deployed recent-level forecast. calibration compares cross-sectional demographic distributions with NHANES. Those records cannot verify an individual's trajectory. A ledger generator and adaptive two-compartment generator feed experiments/run_experiments, summarize and build_report. Inherited raw CSVs, tables, notebooks, models, forecasts and tests are retained.

The existing project already compares OLS, Huber, Theil–Sen, HAC, AR and residual block bootstrap, different windows and error conditions. Its report records 86% coverage for a nominal 95% interval and clean-log cases where BTP wins. Today's work cannot claim these features as new. In the original validator an infinite day can reach integer-span conversion. The qualitative score can warn while still printing a number. Reading-order blocks can span calendar gaps. Both regressions assume a single window slope. A persistent water shift and a tissue trend may look alike.

Plan: independent event-driven observations, separate selection/calibration/final users, robust and Gaussian state-space alternatives, a direct non-identifiability example, and empirical selective risk. Historical results under results/experiments remain a separate benchmark.
